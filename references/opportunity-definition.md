# Opportunity Definition

## S-Level Industrial Inflection

An S-level opportunity is a rare setup where a large industrial expectation curve is likely beginning a first major upward revision and the market has started to confirm it through high-purity assets.

It is not defined by a stock's one-day return. It is defined by the coincidence of:

- Large industrial space.
- Real external catalyst.
- Durable or hard-to-disprove expectation change.
- Early market confirmation.
- High-purity beneficiary assets.
- Reasonable odds at the current price location.
- Bear-case review that does not break the thesis.

## Positive Pattern

The target pattern resembles:

- GPT-4/LLM capability shock causing AI compute and optical module demand to be repriced.
- AI-driven DRAM/HBM tightness starting a memory supercycle, confirmed by overseas memory leaders.
- Trade-war or sanction-risk discount being removed from core AI supply chain assets after a severe fear-driven mispricing.

## What Makes It Different From Normal Theme Trading

Normal theme trading explains "why a sector moved today". This skill asks whether today's move is the first market confirmation of a much larger industrial revision.

An S-level case should answer:

- What did the market know yesterday that is no longer enough today?
- Why can the new expectation persist for weeks or quarters?
- Which assets have direct, high-purity earnings sensitivity?
- What evidence would prove the thesis wrong?

## Minimum Candidate Requirements

A candidate must have:

- A dated catalyst.
- Identifiable beneficiary chain.
- At least one high-grade evidence source.
- Price action in first or early confirmation phase.
- Explicit downside or falsification conditions.

If any of these are missing, the candidate cannot be S.
