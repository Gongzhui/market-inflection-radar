## Industry Background Known By That Date

- AI training and inference systems were increasingly memory-bandwidth constrained. Micron’s March 2025 presentation explicitly framed high-performance processors as requiring more memory bandwidth and positioned HBM as necessary to use those processors effectively.
- NVIDIA’s March 2025 Blackwell Ultra infrastructure messaging referenced DGX GB300 systems scaling to large AI factories, with high-speed memory and networking required for multistep reasoning workloads.
- HBM production consumes more advanced DRAM capacity than standard DRAM for the same bit output. Micron’s March 2025 slides stated HBM3E consumes three times the silicon compared with D5 for the same number of bits.
- SK hynix and Micron were both describing constrained HBM supply / strong AI memory demand before the May 12 trade event. The packet therefore contains two separate evidence buckets: pre-existing AI memory demand/supply evidence and a new macro risk event. The evaluator should test whether the market action is explained by one, the other, or both.
- Standard DRAM was not uniformly strong: consumer DDR4 was still weak in some channels. The differentiated question is whether AI-server, HBM, DDR5, LPDDR server modules, and high-capacity data-center products were tightening enough to change the whole profit curve for memory leaders.

## Bear-Case Material

- The 90-day tariff framework did not permanently settle U.S.-China trade risk.
- Memory is cyclical; price upcycles can reverse if capacity additions overshoot or AI demand slows.
- HBM tightness can be company-specific; Samsung qualification progress or capacity shifts could change the supply picture.
- Consumer DRAM and NAND channels were not uniformly tight; DDR4 weakness and NAND inventory digestion could dilute the broad memory story.
- MU had already been strong in the sessions before 2025-05-12; odds depend on whether the gap still left reasonable upside versus consensus.
- Tariff relief can lift all high-beta semiconductor names; the evaluator must separate sector beta from a memory-specific expectation change.
