## Bear-Case Material

- A small company may lack production capacity, certification, working capital, or customer qualification to deliver large AI server orders.
- If high-purity leaders do not confirm the move, the tape may reflect speculative rotation rather than industry repricing.
- Repeated limit-up behavior can reduce odds even if the theme is real.
