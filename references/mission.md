# Mission

## Core Principle

Upgrade Codex from a "股票异动解释器" to a radar for "产业级预期曲线首次上修并被市场确认".

The skill should surface only rare opportunities worthy of the user's own deep research. It should not recommend stocks daily, explain every abnormal move, or chase forum heat.

## Default Behavior

Default conclusion: `今日无 S 级机会`.

The skill may output an S-level report only when the candidate simultaneously passes all seven gates in `scoring-rubric.md`.

## What This Skill Is Looking For

Look for situations where:

- A new external catalyst materially changes the forward demand, supply, pricing, policy, or risk-discount curve of a large industry.
- The market confirms that change with a first or early strong price reaction in high-purity beneficiaries.
- The thesis has not already been fully priced by a long, crowded move.
- The opportunity can plausibly become a multi-quarter or multi-year earnings revision cycle, not merely a one-day trade.

## Non-Goals

Do not:

- Produce daily stock recommendations.
- Explain every limit-up, gap-up, or sector rotation.
- Rate low-quality rumor clusters as S.
- Treat policy slogans as earnings evidence.
- Treat "news heat" or "forum heat" as proof of industrial inflection.
- Output S without a high-grade evidence chain and a documented bear-case review.

## Expected Output Frequency

Most trading days should end with no S-level opportunity. A conservative false negative is preferable to a false positive that trains the user to chase noise.
