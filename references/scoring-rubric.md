# Scoring Rubric

This skill uses hard gates, not a weighted average. A candidate cannot compensate for a failed core gate with high scores elsewhere.

## Rating Scale

- `S`: all seven gates pass, anti-hype checklist passes, and evidence quality supports the core thesis.
- `A`: strong candidate with one non-fatal missing element, usually awaiting confirmation or better price location.
- `B`: interesting theme or early clue, but no complete industrial inflection.
- `C`: ordinary rotation, weak evidence, rumor-driven move, or no investable high-purity asset.
- `Reject`: hype, manipulation risk, stale move, or no falsifiable thesis.

## Seven Gates

### Gate 1: Huge Industry Space

Pass only if the affected market can plausibly support multi-quarter or multi-year revenue and earnings revisions.

Evidence can include TAM expansion, hyperscaler capex, industry pricing cycle, capacity shortage, order backlog, policy budget, or global leader guidance.

Fail if the story is narrow, one-company only, or too small to reprice a chain.

### Gate 2: Short-Term Hard to Disprove

Pass only if the thesis has near-term narrative durability and cannot be immediately invalidated by obvious data.

Examples: long qualification cycles, capacity shortages, visible order backlog, large capex cycles, early adoption curve, or policy execution window.

Fail if the thesis depends on an imminent binary result, a single unverified rumor, or already-known data.

### Gate 3: Real External Catalyst

Pass only if a dated outside event changed expectations.

Acceptable catalysts include company filings, earnings calls, major product releases, confirmed orders, policy documents, industry price data, overseas leader guidance, or risk-removal events.

Fail if the catalyst is only "涨得多", "讨论热", or recycled old information.

### Gate 4: First or Early Large Bullish Confirmation

Pass only if high-purity assets show first or early confirmation: gap-up, large bullish candle, breakout from fear discount, strong volume, or cross-market leader confirmation.

Fail if the move is late-stage, already repeatedly accelerated, or only back-row catch-up after the core assets have run.

### Gate 5: High-Purity Beneficiary Assets

Pass only if there are identifiable assets with direct revenue, margin, capacity, valuation, or risk-discount sensitivity.

Tier assets:

- `Core`: direct revenue or margin sensitivity, industry leadership, and liquid market confirmation.
- `Mapped`: credible supply-chain or overseas-to-domestic mapping, but with some uncertainty.
- `Peripheral`: theme exposure without enough earnings purity.

S requires at least one Core asset.

### Gate 6: Reasonable Odds Position

Pass only if the current price location still leaves asymmetric research value after considering drawdown, valuation, crowding, and prior gains.

Fail if the only remaining opportunity is late momentum chasing, especially after repeated climax moves.

### Gate 7: Bear-Case Review Not Broken

Pass only after explicitly checking the strongest opposing explanations.

Common bear cases:

- The move is short covering or index beta.
- The catalyst was known and priced.
- Earnings transmission is too weak.
- Supply response will erase pricing power.
- Policy/order evidence is not real.
- Beneficiary purity is low.

If a bear case destroys the core thesis, cap at B or lower.

## Automatic Rating Caps

- Low-grade evidence as core support: maximum B.
- No high-purity Core asset: maximum B.
- No real external catalyst: maximum C.
- No first or early market confirmation: maximum B.
- Late back-row补涨 after continuous sector climax: maximum C.
- Pure policy小作文 without official source or order path: maximum C.
- Forum rumor as main driver: maximum C.
- No clear earnings or valuation transmission path: maximum B.
