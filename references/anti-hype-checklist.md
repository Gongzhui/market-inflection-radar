# Anti-Hype Checklist

Run this checklist after building the positive case. Any hard block prevents an S rating.

## Hard Blocks

Do not rate S merely because:

- Single-day gain is large.
- News heat is high.
- Forum discussion is hot.
- A low-market-cap stock has consecutive limit-up moves.
- A policy 小作文 is circulating without official source or earnings transmission.
- Back-row stocks are catching up after the core assets have already risen continuously.
- The theme name is fashionable but beneficiary purity is low.
- A stock is "small cap + elastic" without a visible revenue, margin, or order path.
- The market move can be fully explained by beta, short covering, index inclusion, or liquidity.
- The catalyst is stale and was known before the move.

## Required Skeptical Questions

Ask:

- What would have been different if this were only a normal rotation?
- Is the strongest evidence primary or merely repeated commentary?
- Are core assets moving first, or only peripheral names?
- Has the market already repriced the same thesis for several sessions?
- Can the catalyst change earnings, supply-demand, valuation, or risk discount?
- What data would disprove the thesis within the next 1-8 weeks?

## Rating Impact

- Any hard block: maximum B, often C.
- Multiple hard blocks: Reject.
- Checklist passed but one gate weak: maximum A.
- Checklist passed and all seven gates pass: S is allowed, not automatic; still write the full evidence table and bear case.
